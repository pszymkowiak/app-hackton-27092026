ONE-PAGER: THE IDEA
Pourwise — scan it, get the exact volume, get it delivered
PROBLEM
Ordering ready-mix concrete is broken, for pros and individuals alike.
- Nobody knows the exact volume. Contractors estimate by hand and add a big safety margin; homeowners guess. Concrete is perishable (about 90 minutes once batched), so running short is a disaster and over-ordering is the norm.
- Over 125 million tonnes of fresh concrete are returned to plants every year worldwide. In the US, around 5% of production comes back.
- A single rejected truck costs a contractor around $1,500 and up to 3 tonnes of CO2.
- There is no easy way to compare suppliers. Orders go by phone, prices are opaque, and small buyers are often ignored.
SOLUTION
A worldwide platform that connects concrete producers with the people who need concrete delivered: contractors (B2B) and individuals (B2C).
1. Scan: the customer takes photos of the slab, footing or wall and adds a voice note. Gemini computes the exact volume, with a justified margin instead of a guess. Missing dimensions become a short question.
2. Choose the concrete: the app suggests the standard mix for the use (patio, driveway, foundation). It never invents structural specs: for engineered work, the customer enters the spec from their plans.
3. Compare and order: nearby plants that can deliver within the concrete's time window show their price and delivery slots. The customer picks the best offer and pays in the app.
4. Share a truck (flexible delivery): customers who are not in a hurry tick "I can wait" and pay less. Pourwise pools their order with others nearby into one truckload, or fills it with the surplus from a larger pour in the same area. Fuller trucks, fewer trips, lower price.
5. Deliver: the plant receives a complete, confirmed order, and a readiness check before dispatch keeps trucks from waiting on an unready site.

BUSINESS MODEL
Two revenue streams:
1. Annual listing fee: concrete producers pay a fixed yearly fee to be referenced on Pourwise ($1,200 per plant per year). Predictable, recurring revenue from the supply side.
2. Transaction fee: 5% of every order placed through the platform, capped at $99, so large contractor orders stay cheap and pros have no reason to go around the platform.
Free for customers to scan and compare.
WHY PLANTS JOIN
New customers they don't reach today, full trucks thanks to shared deliveries, a buyer for surplus concrete instead of washing it out, and orders that arrive complete with the right volume, so fewer returns.
IMPACT
Every cubic meter not over-ordered saves the buyer money, saves the plant a returned load, and avoids 250-480 kg of CO2. Ordering from the nearest able plant, and sharing trucks between flexible customers, cuts truck kilometers.
MARKET AND SCALE
Ready-mix concrete is one of the largest building-material markets in the world, and every order starts with a volume estimate. Worldwide from day one: voice-first and multilingual, and plants anywhere can list. Next: asphalt, aggregates and other bulk site materials on the same platform.
